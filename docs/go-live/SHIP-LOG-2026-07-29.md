## Two live defects found and fixed during the ship

Both would have failed silently in production.

1. **`verify_jwt` was on for the roll-call trio.** `roll-call-ack`, `commitment-reminders` and
   `commitment-escalation` need it off — the first exists precisely so an athlete can ack from the
   lock screen with no session, and the other two are cron-invoked. They were missing from
   `config.toml`, so the default won and each was rejected at the platform layer before its own
   auth ran. Now pinned in `config.toml` (`54e0941`) so a redeploy can't reintroduce it.

2. **The documented way to schedule the escalation ladder destroyed the reminder job.**
   `ROLLCALL-LOCKSCREEN.md` said to call `schedule_commitment_reminders()` a second time with the
   escalation URL. That function hardcodes `jobname = 'commitment-reminders'` and unschedules
   before rescheduling, so the second call replaces reminders rather than adding the ladder. The
   result is silent and inverted: reminders stop, while the ladder that only chases people who
   ignored a reminder keeps running — and `cron.job` still shows one healthy row. Migration
   **0159** gives escalation its own scheduler and job name; the doc now carries the warning
   (`63dac07`).
