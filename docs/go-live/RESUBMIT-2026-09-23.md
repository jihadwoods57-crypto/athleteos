```
OnStandard is an accountability app for athletes and the coaches, trainers and parents who work with them. Athletes photograph meals and do a nightly check-in; a coach can run a morning roll call.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 1.0 (33)
- 5.1.1(iv) Priming screens use "Continue", never "Allow", and do not tell the user which option to pick. Notifications and alarms are asked from a Continue card, never at launch.
- 2.5.4 No background modes at all. This build uses location again, for one optional feature, below.
- 2.1(b) Four subscriptions, priced in the store's local currency; the purchase screen buys them.
- 5.1.2(i) Third-party AI is disclosed and asked before any data is sent (below).

DEMO ACCOUNTS (connected to each other)
- Athlete: review-athlete@onstandard.app / [PASSWORD]
- Coach: review-coach@onstandard.app / [PASSWORD] (team code CLUQPB)
Use the athlete for HealthKit, membership, roll call and location. The first AI action shows a permission screen: tap Continue.

ROLL CALL (AlarmKit)
A coach assigns a morning roll call to the team from one screen. The athlete allows alarms once, at team join (Not now works too; Settings can turn alarms off anytime). OnStandard sets a real alarm per assigned morning, coach-assigned only, when the app opens, and updates or removes it on a move or cancel, notifying the athlete each time. If the app was never opened before start, a time-sensitive backup alert sounds instead. No background modes. Alarm buttons check the athlete in and open the team board.

LOCATION (When In Use, optional Always)
- Used only for a roll call the coach gave a place, only inside its check-in window.
- When In Use: the athlete taps "I'm here". One reading goes to the server, which compares it with the coach's place, keeps only Arrived / Not arrived, and discards the coordinate. Nobody sees where anyone is.
- Always (optional): the app uses Core Location region monitoring for one circular region during the window and removes it at the close. iOS wakes the app on entry; one reading is sent. No continuous tracking, no background location mode.
- Declining either keeps the app fully usable; "I'm here" or the coach's manual mark work instead.
- To see the prompts: step 1 with a place at your current location, then open the roll call as the athlete.

DICTATION
In a meal's conversation, tap the mic in the message box. iOS asks for Microphone and Speech Recognition then, not before. Speech becomes editable text; nothing is sent until Send; no audio is stored.

HEALTHKIT
Athlete: Profile > Tracking > Apple Health. Read-only: sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, steps, distance, exercise minutes, active energy, workouts. Never written, never used for ads. Not available on iPad; please use an iPhone for live data.

AI (5.1.2(i))
Meal photos, chat messages and the profile facts the AI uses are analyzed by Anthropic (Claude). Before the first photo or message is sent, the app names the provider and asks; "Not now" still logs meals by photo, without the AI read. Minors get no AI until a guardian approves.

IN-APP PURCHASE
Athlete: Profile > Plan & billing > See membership plans. Individual and Family, monthly or annual, 14-day trial for eligible accounts, terms, privacy, Restore. Team plans are organization accounts sold outside the app (3.1.3(c)); the app does not say where or how to buy them.

ACCOUNT
Sign in with Apple, Google or email. New Apple/Google accounts go through date of birth first. Profile > Preferences > Delete account deletes server-side and revokes Apple sign-in tokens.

USER CONTENT (1.2)
Chats are visible only to people connected to the athlete. "In this conversation" lists each person with Report and Block. Reports email our support inbox within minutes. support@onstandard.app

MINORS
Date of birth is required for every new account. Under 13 refused; 13-17 need guardian approval before data is shared. Rated 13+, not Kids.

Privacy: onstandard.app/privacy. No tracking, no ad SDKs.
```

## 4. Decided for you in the review pass (tell me if any is wrong)

- **Coach plans on iPhone:** not sold in the app. The app says only "Team plans aren't sold in
  the app." Room and School tiers are deferred.
- **Sign-up:** a plain "You start free" step; the only place to buy is the membership screen.
- **Coach status words:** one word per state everywhere (Overdue, Below standard, On standard…).
  This reverses the 09-22 rule where a 71 read "Building".
- **Unknown carbs, fat and fiber** stay unknown: no "Fat in range", no fake zero. If the AI reads
  only protein and calories, the meal logs with the rest unknown.
- **Instagram/X links** removed from Profile until you confirm the handles.
- **Copilot screen** removed (nothing linked to it).
- **Group threads:** the AI only reads messages from people who agreed to AI. Other people's
  messages reach it as "[a message from the coach]".
- **Minors:** no AI until a parent approves (the privacy policy promised this).
- **Every current user** sees the AI permission screen once. AI stays off for each person until
  they tap Continue.
