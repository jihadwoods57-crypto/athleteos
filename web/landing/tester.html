What you're testing, in plain English

OnStandard answers one question for athletes and the people around them: is this athlete actually doing what they're supposed to? An athlete takes a photo of each meal, the app reads it and scores it, they do a short nightly check-in, and it all rolls into one daily score out of 100. Their coach, trainer, or parent can see that score, so the work is visible instead of taken on faith.

Your job is simple: use it like it's real for a few days and tell us everything. The app isn't public yet, so what you catch now gets fixed before anyone else sees it.

  1. Install the app through TestFlight

    The app isn't on the App Store yet. Apple's way of sharing pre-release apps is a free Apple app called TestFlight. Install TestFlight from the App Store first, then tap the button below and accept the invite. OnStandard installs from inside TestFlight and works like any other app after that.

  2. Live as the athlete

    Sign in with the athlete email above. Go through the setup questions, then do the real thing: photograph an actual meal you eat, answer the nightly check-in, watch the score move. This is the everyday experience, so spend most of your time here.

  3. See what the coach sees

    Sign out (the Profile tab in the bottom corner, then Sign out), and sign back in as the coach. That meal you just logged? It's on this side too. This is how a coach watches a whole team without texting "did you eat?" twenty times a day.

  4. Try the trainer and client pair

    Same idea, different world: a personal trainer and their client instead of a team and its athletes. Log something as the client, then look at it as the trainer.

  5. Report everything you notice

    Confusing, broken, slow, ugly, or great: it all counts, and the small stuff counts double. The Report anything button below goes to our feedback board, tagged with your tester number, so nothing gets lost in a text thread.
