1. Who this policy is for

OnStandard is an athlete nutrition-accountability platform for athletes and the people who support them (parents/guardians, personal trainers, sports-performance coaches, nutritionists, and high-school and college coaches). The app's primary users are athletes roughly ages 13–22. Some athletes are minors. We treat athlete nutrition, weight, and check-in data as sensitive and apply heightened protections to minors' data (see §7 and §8).

2. Two modes: on-device vs. connected

  • On-device only. When the app's backend is not enabled, the app runs entirely on your device using local storage. In this state, no personal data is sent to or collected by our servers.
  • Connected (backend enabled). When the backend is enabled and you sign in, your data is stored on our hosted infrastructure so it can sync across your devices and be shared with the people you have linked, subject to the consent rules in §7.

3. What we collect (connected state)

  • Account and identity: name, email, role (athlete / parent / trainer / coach / nutritionist), and — for athletes — sport, position, school or program/organization name, primary goal, training frequency, and the age you enter at setup.
  • Nutrition and health data: meals you log and their estimated or transcribed macros; meal photos and Nutrition Facts label photos you capture (see §4); daily accountability scores; body weight, starting weight, and weight goals; hydration; weekly check-in answers; and performance records (PRs) you log.
  • Relationships and links: the coaches, trainers, nutritionists, and guardians you link to; team/join codes; and, for staff accounts, the athletes on your roster or client book.
  • Messages you send to linked people through the app, including text you dictate (see §3b).
  • Authentication data: your sign-in is handled by our authentication provider; we store your account identifier and email. We never store your password in the app. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email that provider shares; for Sign in with Apple we also keep a token that lets us revoke the Apple sign-in when you delete your account.
  • Payment and subscription data (paid accounts only): for a coach, trainer, or organization on a paid plan, we and our payment processor keep your billing email, the plan, your subscription status and renewal date, and the processor's customer and subscription identifiers. Card numbers are entered directly with our payment processor and never reach our servers. For an individual or family membership bought in the app, Apple bills you under the App Store's terms and we never see your payment details; our subscription platform (RevenueCat) tells us only your plan, its status and renewal date, keyed to your account identifier. Athletes and clients attached to a paying account pay nothing and provide no payment data.
  • Notifications: if you enable reminders or digests, we store a device push token so we can deliver them. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or your device settings.
  • Technical and security data: we process your IP address transiently to rate-limit and protect the service from abuse (for example, to cap automated requests). We use it for security, not to build a profile of you or track you across apps or sites.
  • Apple Health (iPhone, optional, read-only): if you choose to connect Apple Health, the app reads last night's sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, steps, walking and running distance, and workouts, with your permission, so your recovery check-in has real context. We never write to Apple Health. If your coach sets an activity standard (for example a step or exercise-minute goal), your activity totals for that standard (steps, distance, exercise minutes, active energy, workouts) are shared with that coach so they can see your progress toward it; the rest stays with you. Health data is used only to show you and your linked coach the check-in context you already see; it is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties, never used to make eligibility or insurance decisions, and is not stored in iCloud. You can disconnect at any time in the app (Preferences › Health) or in the iOS Health app, and we stop reading immediately.
  • Location: only for a roll call your coach gave a place, and only if you allow it. See §3a.
  • We do NOT collect advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking data, or analytics from third-party advertising/tracking SDKs, and we never use location for advertising or to track you across apps or sites. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-tracking SDKs.

3a. Location check-in (roll call)

A coach can give a roll call a place (a weight room, a practice field). For that roll call only, OnStandard can confirm that you got there.

  • What is read, and when. Your phone takes one location reading when you tap "I'm here", or, if you chose walk-in check-in, when you walk into the place. Only during that roll call's check-in window. Outside a window nothing is read.
  • What is kept. The reading goes to our server once, which compares it with the coach's place and keeps only the verdict, Arrived or Not arrived, and how far away you were. The coordinate itself is discarded.
  • Who sees it. Your coach and your team see Arrived or Not arrived on the roll call board. Only you see how far away you were. Nobody sees where you are.
  • Walk-in check-in (optional). With "Always" permission, the phone watches one circular region around the place during the window (iOS region monitoring) and removes it when the window closes. There is no continuous tracking and no background location mode.
  • Minors. For an athlete under 18, location check-in stays off until a parent or guardian approves it.
  • Switching it off. Turn off walk-in check-in in the app (Profile › Location check-in) or in your phone's Settings. Declining location never costs you anything else: your coach can mark you in.

3b. Microphone and speech

  • If you tap the microphone in a message box, the app asks for the microphone and speech recognition then, not before. Your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, on your device where the iPhone supports it and otherwise by Apple's speech service, under Apple's terms.
  • The text appears in the message box for you to edit. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and no audio is stored by OnStandard or sent to our servers.

4. Meal analysis by a third-party AI (Anthropic / Claude), with your permission

OnStandard's AI Nutritionist is provided by Anthropic (the Claude API). Before any of your data is sent to it, the app asks you, in a screen that names Anthropic and lists what is sent, with Continue and Not now. Your answer is recorded on your account, and our server refuses to send your data to the AI without it. You can change it at any time in the app (Profile › Privacy & visibility › AI).

  • What is sent, when AI is on: the meal and Nutrition Facts label photos you take, what you write in a meal conversation, and the facts the AI coaches from: your goal, weight goal, position, allergies, and your coach's standard. For a coach, trainer or dietitian, the questions they ask the AI about an athlete's meal; an athlete's own data is sent only if that athlete has said yes too. In a shared meal conversation, a message from anyone who has not said yes is never sent: the AI sees only "[a message from the coach]" (or parent, and so on), with no words and no name.
  • Short summaries, under the same setting: some features send already-computed text and numbers, never photos, to reword or summarize them: the coach-voice nudge on your Home screen (today's progress), the weekly deep dive and the monthly report (your own history), and your coach's written read of your last two weeks. The app never lets the AI change a number, and none of these run unless AI is on for you.
  • If you choose Not now: everything else keeps working. A meal photo still counts as proof, you can log numbers yourself, and the AI stays quiet. Scheduled AI features (the daily follow-up, the coach's summary) skip you.
  • The consent gate fails closed: no answer means no AI. For an athlete under 18, AI stays off until a parent or guardian has approved the account, even if the athlete says yes. Once there is an account our server enforces this; before sign-up (the onboarding demo), the app does not send the photo of anyone who gave an age under 18.
  • The Anthropic API key is held only on our server; it is never in the app.
  • We use the AI to provide the nutrition feature. We do not use your photos for advertising.

Under Anthropic’s commercial API terms, data we submit (including meal photos) is not used to train Anthropic’s models, and API inputs and outputs are retained only for a limited period for trust-and-safety monitoring before deletion.

5. Not medical advice

OnStandard provides nutrition education and accountability, not medical advice. AI meal estimates, scores, and coaching copy are estimates and may be inaccurate. The app is not a substitute for a physician or registered dietitian. If you or someone you support is struggling with eating or body image, help is available: in the U.S., the National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline at 1-866-662-1235, or call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).

6. How we use your data

We use your data to provide the service: analyze and log meals, compute and show your accountability score and trends, let the people you have linked see what you have agreed to share, generate roster/compliance views for your coach or trainer, and send the reminders you enable. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Who can see your data, and your sharing controls

  • You can always see and export your own data.
  • People you link can see the athlete data you have consented to share — e.g. your daily score, compliance, and weight trend, and, for an activity standard your coach sets, your activity totals.
  • Teammates see your roll call answers on the team's roll call board (your name, when you answered, your place in line, and Arrived or Not arrived when the roll call has a place). Your score appears on the Squad board only if you opt in. Teammates never see your meals, photos, weight, check-ins or location.
  • Blocking. You can block anyone in a conversation. Their messages are hidden from you and their notifications stop; they are not told.
  • Consent is required and revocable. Real athlete data is only collected and shared after consent is recorded. You can pause all sharing at any time from the Profile screen, which immediately stops data leaving the device, and you can remove a linked viewer's access.
  • Service providers (subprocessors): our hosting/database/storage and authentication provider (Supabase), our email provider (Resend) for account emails, Anthropic for the AI analysis in §4, Stripe for payment processing on paid plans, RevenueCat for in-app subscription status (plan, status and renewal date only; Apple handles payment), and Expo for delivering the push notifications you enable. Each operates under contract and may process data only to provide its service to us.
  • Legal disclosure: we may disclose data if required by law or to protect users (especially minors).

8. Minors and parental/guardian consent

  • Under 13 are barred. The minimum signup age is 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if we learn we have, we will delete it.
  • Ages 13–17 require verified guardian consent before any data is shared. For an athlete under 18, the app keeps the athlete's real data on the device only until a parent or guardian is verifiably approved. A self-tapped checkbox by the minor is not sufficient; the gate fails closed.
  • Minor safety in messaging. A registered minor cannot be placed in an unsupervised message thread with an unrelated adult; minor messaging is limited to the minor's authorized coach, trainer, or guardian.
  • Guardian rights. A parent or guardian can review their minor's data, request its deletion, and withdraw consent at any time using the in-app controls or by contacting support@onstandard.app.

9. Education records (FERPA)

Where a school, team, or coach provides rosters, or where OnStandard handles data that may constitute education records, we handle that data to provide the service and do not sell it or use it for unrelated purposes.

10. Your rights: access, export, and deletion

  • Export. You can export a structured (JSON) copy of your own data from the Account screen.
  • Deletion. You can permanently delete your account and data from the app. Deletion removes your server account and cascades to delete the data you own (days, meals, check-ins, links, threads) and your meal-photo storage, and wipes your local on-device data. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke that sign-in with Apple.
  • GDPR / CCPA-CPRA. EU/UK and California residents have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, portability, and opt-out of sale/sharing). We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used by CCPA/CPRA. To exercise any right, use the in-app controls or email support@onstandard.app.

11. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data and meal photos as described in §10, and complete deletion within 30 days.

12. Security

  • The app stores your authenticated session token in the device's encrypted OS keychain, not in plain storage.
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and the database enforces row-level security so that only you and the people you have linked can read your data.
  • The app bundle contains no secret keys. The public API key shipped in the app is a publishable key gated by server-side row-level security; the Anthropic key lives only on the server.
  • Meal photos are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and to Anthropic for analysis, where they are processed for that purpose. No system is perfectly secure.

13. International transfers

OnStandard operates from the United States, and our service providers (including Supabase and Anthropic) process data in the U.S. If you use the Service from outside the U.S., you understand that your information is transferred to and processed in the U.S. Where applicable law requires a transfer mechanism, we rely on our providers’ standard contractual protections.

We will post changes here and update the effective date. We will provide notice of material changes; material changes affecting minors will be communicated to guardians.
